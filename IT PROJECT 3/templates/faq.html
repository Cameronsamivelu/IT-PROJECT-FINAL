Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ANGEL and luggage tracking.

What is ANGEL?

ANGEL stands for Airport Navigation & Guidance for Every Luggage. It is an airport luggage tracking system designed to help passengers check the status and location of their baggage.

What is the purpose of ANGEL?

ANGEL aims to make luggage tracking easier and more convenient. Passengers can check their baggage information, view tracking updates and access assistance when needed.

How do I track my luggage?

Visit the ANGEL homepage and enter your luggage tracking ID in the "Track Your Luggage" search box. Click Search to view the available information about your baggage.

What if I forget my password?

If you forget your password, return to the ANGEL homepage and open the ANGEL chatbot to request support. Do not share your password with anyone through the chat.

What if my luggage is delayed or lost?

Use your tracking ID to check the latest available baggage status. If your luggage is delayed or marked as lost, contact ANGEL support for further guidance.

How can I contact ANGEL support?

Open the ANGEL chatbot on the homepage to access automated assistance. Additional support options may be available through the chatbot.

Do I need an account to track my luggage?

You can use the luggage tracking search on the ANGEL homepage with a valid tracking ID. A customer account may provide additional features.
