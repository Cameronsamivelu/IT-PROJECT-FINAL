How ANGEL works

From luggage registration to checking its recorded journey information, ANGEL brings the main steps together.

01

Register the luggage

Luggage details are entered through the available customer or employee registration process.

02

Keep the tracking ID

A successful registration generates a unique ANGEL tracking reference that can be used to look up the saved luggage record.

03

Review journey updates

Enter the tracking reference to view recorded status and location information. Customers can sign in to view their linked luggage history.

Make ANGEL part of your journey.

Create a customer account to access your linked luggage history, or sign in if you already have an account. You can also look up luggage using a tracking ID above.
